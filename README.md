<b>🛡️ BhashaKavach  </b>  
Bhasha + Kavach — Regional Language Phishing Detection & Protection  
BhashaKavach is a proposed multilingual phishing detection web application designed to identify and warn users about phishing attempts in regional languages, helping make cybersecurity more accessible to non-technical and regional-language users.  

  
<b>🚨Problem -</b>  
- Phishing is commonly delivered through SMS, emails, messaging platforms, fake websites, and malicious URLs.
- Many phishing attempts use regional languages, transliterated text, or mixed-language content to target users.
- Existing phishing detection approaches often focus heavily on English-centric content.
- Users who are less comfortable with English may find it harder to recognize suspicious messages.
- This creates a language accessibility gap in cybersecurity.

<b>How can we provide an accessible phishing detection mechanism capable of analyzing regional-language and multilingual content and warning users before they fall victim to a phishing attack?</b>

<b>💡 Proposed Solution -</b>  
We introduce our web app BhashaKavach.  
<b>What does it do?</b>
- Accept suspicious messages/text or URLs from the user.
- Analyze the submitted content.
- Identify phishing indicators.
- Consider regional-language/multilingual characteristics.
- Classify the content based on its risk.
- Provide an understandable warning to the user.
<b>🎯 Objectives</b>
- Detect phishing attempts in regional and multilingual content.
- Reduce the language barrier in cybersecurity.
- Help non-technical users identify suspicious messages and URLs.
- Provide clear and understandable security warnings.
- Promote safer digital communication.
- Build a scalable foundation for supporting multiple Indian languages.

<b>✨ Key Features</b>  
🌐 Multilingual Analysis  
🔗 URL Detection  
📝 Message Analysis  
⚠️ Risk Classification  
💬 Explainable Warning

<b>🌟 Innovation & Uniqueness</b>  
Language-Aware Cybersecurity  
Instead of focusing only on conventional English phishing content, BhashaKavach focuses on the regional-language dimension of phishing.  
Accessibility  
Makes phishing detection easier for users who primarily communicate in Indian languages.  
Explainable Detection  
The system aims to tell users why something appears suspicious.  
Scalable Architecture  
The architecture can be extended to additional Indian languages and future detection techniques.
<b>👥 Target Users</b>  
Possible target users:  
- Regional-language internet users
- Students
- Elderly users
- First-time digital users
- Non-technical users
- Digital payment users
- Users receiving suspicious SMS/messages
- Users who primarily communicate in Indian languages
